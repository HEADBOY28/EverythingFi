Everything you need to use Jabiru

A short guide to what Jabiru is, how to find the right app fast, and answers to the questions we hear most.

Introduction

Jabiru is a curated directory of Web3 apps, sorted by category so you can find the right tool in seconds.

Web3 moves fast. New tools launch every week, and finding a trustworthy one often means digging through threads, group chats and search results. Jabiru puts them in one place: each app has a short description, its category, and a direct link to its official website.

How Jabiru works

Five steps from the home page to the app you need.

  1. Get started

    On the home page, choose how to enter. Connect wallet links your wallet address. Continue with email signs you up for updates. Explore apps lets you browse with no account at all. Every option gives you the same full directory.

  2. Browse by category

    Each category is a folder. Open one to see every app inside it, or pick a category from the side menu (the ☰ button on phones). Use All to see everything at once.

  3. Search

    Type in the search bar to match app names, descriptions, categories and tags. For example, try "bridge", "Solana" or "whale".

  4. Filter

    Click Filters to narrow results by pricing, blockchain, topic or format. Combine as many filters as you like. Only apps that match all of them are shown.

  5. Open an app

    Click an app card to open its official website in a new tab. Jabiru stays open, so you can come back and compare.

Filters and tags

Tags describe each app so you can filter precisely. They come in four groups:

GroupWhat it tells you
PricingFree, Freemium (free plan with paid upgrades) or Paid. Trading and network fees don't count as paid.
EcosystemThe blockchain an app focuses on, such as Ethereum, Solana, Base, Bitcoin or Hyperliquid.
TopicWhat the app does, such as Whale Tracking, Rug Check, Yield, Portfolio or Taxes.
FormatHow you use it: Telegram Bot, Browser Extension, Mobile App or Open Source.

Filtered views are saved in the page link, so you can bookmark them or share them with a friend.

🛡️ Staying safe
  • Always open apps from Jabiru or their official site, not from links in DMs.
  • Jabiru will never ask for your seed phrase, private key or a transaction signature.
  • Read every wallet prompt before approving it, and start with small amounts on new apps.
  • A listing is not an endorsement. Do your own research before using any app.

Frequently asked questions

General
Is Jabiru free?

Yes. Browsing, searching and filtering are completely free. Some listed apps charge for their own services, and their Pricing tag shows this.

Do I need an account?

No. Choose Explore apps on the home page to browse without connecting a wallet or giving an email.

How are apps chosen?

The Jabiru team reviews and adds each app by hand, linking to its official website. Being listed does not mean we endorse an app, so always do your own research.

Are any links referral links?

A small number of links include a referral code. They cost you nothing extra and help support Jabiru.

Does Jabiru work on mobile?

Yes. The whole site works on phones. To connect a wallet on mobile, open Jabiru inside your wallet app's built-in browser, such as MetaMask.

Wallet and email
What happens when I connect my wallet?

Jabiru only reads your public wallet address. It never asks you to sign a transaction, approve spending or share your seed phrase, and it cannot move your funds.

If any site claiming to be Jabiru asks for those, close it.

What do you do with my email?

Your email is stored privately and used to send Jabiru news, new apps and offers. You only receive these if you tick the box when signing up.

How do I unsubscribe or delete my email?

Use the unsubscribe link in any of our emails, or message @Jabiru_ on X and we'll remove your address.

How do I sign out?

On the home page, click Disconnect (wallet) or Sign out (email). This clears your sign-in from this browser.

Apps and listings
How do I suggest an app?

Send the app's name and website to @Jabiru_ on X. We review every suggestion.

I found a broken link or wrong information. What should I do?

Message @Jabiru_ on X with the app name and the problem, and we'll fix it.

Can I share a category or filtered view?

Yes. Categories and filters are saved in the page link. Copy it from your browser's address bar and share it.
