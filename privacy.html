Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Jabiru collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 12 November 2026
TL;DR
  • You can browse Jabiru without giving us any personal information.
  • If you sign up with email, we store your address to send you Jabiru news, new apps and offers.
  • Your wallet address stays in your browser. We never see your private keys and cannot move your funds.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies, and we don't sell your data.

1. Who we are

Jabiru (“Jabiru”, “we”, “us”) is a curated directory of Web3 apps available at this website. This policy applies to the Jabiru website and its pages, including the home page, the app directory and the docs.

You can contact us about privacy at any time by messaging @Jabiru_ on X.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Email address. If you choose Continue with email, we store your email address, the date you signed up and a record that you agreed to receive updates.
  • Messages. If you contact us on X, we receive whatever you choose to send.

Information stored only in your browser

  • Wallet address. When you connect a wallet, Jabiru reads your public wallet address and saves it in your browser so you stay connected. It is not sent to our servers.
  • Preferences. Your theme (light or dark) and whether you are signed in are saved in your browser’s local storage.

Technical information

  • Abuse prevention. When you submit the email form, we keep a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address for up to one hour to limit spam. We never store your IP address itself.
  • Hosting logs. Our hosting provider may process standard request data, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to deliver and secure the site.

We do not use analytics tools, advertising trackers or tracking cookies.

3. How we use your information

  • To send you Jabiru news, new apps and offers, if you agreed to receive them.
  • To run, maintain and secure Jabiru, including preventing spam and abuse.
  • To reply to your messages and requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. Legal basis

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on your consent to send you marketing emails, and on our legitimate interests to keep the site secure and working. You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run Jabiru, and only as needed:

  • Vercel, which hosts the website.
  • Upstash, which stores the email list in a private database.
  • An email delivery service, if we use one to send our emails.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the site’s fonts and receives your IP address when the page loads.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of Jabiru and its users.

6. Third-party apps

Jabiru links to apps and websites run by other companies. When you open one, you leave Jabiru, and that app’s own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for how third parties handle your data, so please review their policies before using them.

7. How long we keep information

  • Email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Hashed IP addresses used for spam prevention are deleted automatically within one hour.
  • Information in your browser stays until you sign out, disconnect or clear your browser data.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe using the link in any of our emails.
  • Access or delete your email address by messaging @Jabiru_ on X.
  • Sign out or disconnect on the home page to remove your sign-in from your browser.
  • Clear your browser data to remove everything Jabiru saved on your device.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to correct your data, object to or restrict its use, receive a copy of it, or complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including storing emails in a private database that is not publicly accessible. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

Jabiru is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us their email, contact @Jabiru_ and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we may also let you know by email or on the site.

13. Contact us

For any privacy questions or requests, message @Jabiru_ on X.
